Now partnered with LearnChess Academy

Exciting new developments for junior players: structured coaching, a clear path to improve, and proper tuition alongside the club night. If your child has an interest in chess, this is the place to start.

01

Learn the game properly

From how the pieces move to real openings, tactics and endgames — taught in the right order, at the right pace.

02

Play real games

Games against other juniors every week, with helpful advice on hand whenever it's wanted.

03

Move up to the senior club

When they're ready, juniors join the adult club night, the ladder and eventually our league teams.
