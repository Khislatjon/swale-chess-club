Thursday nights in Sittingbourne

Chess for everyone, from novice to expert.

We meet every Thursday evening at The Artisan Room. Club championships, rapid tournaments, a members' ladder and league matches across Kent — plus plenty of casual games for anyone who just fancies a game.

What we play

Four ways to get a game

Whatever you're after — a serious season-long campaign or a friendly game and a chat — there's a place for you on a Thursday.

Club Championship

Our main all-play-all competition, running from autumn through to summer. Keith Nevols is the reigning 2025–26 champion.

Rapid & blitz

A group-stage rapid championship feeding into knockout quarter-finals, plus a blitz night to close the season.

The ladder

Challenge anyone near you on the ladder and climb. The easiest way in if you'd rather not commit to a full tournament.

League & county

We field teams in the Kent leagues, and members play for the county — Kent won the U1650 national final in 2026.

Swale Junior Chess Club

The ultimate level playground

Chess can be played on equal terms by everyone — there's no advantage in being a girl or a boy, young or old, large or small. Our junior club meets on Thursdays from 6.15pm to 7.30pm and caters for every young player, from complete novice to budding world champion.

Now partnered with LearnChess Academy. Structured coaching and a clear path to improve, alongside the club night.

Join us

New members always welcome

Come along on any Thursday from 7.40pm — no need to book, and no need to be any good. Bring nothing but yourself; we have the boards, clocks and sets.

Annual subscription is £96, which includes your registration with the English Chess Federation so you can play rated games and league matches.
